## The short version

- **AI Wizard Duel is public.** Your wizards, spells, duels (including campaign battles), results and profile can be seen by anyone, live and in replays. Duel chat can be seen by any signed-in viewer. Don't post personal information.
- **Some things stay private:** your email, payment details, token balance, notifications and the devices you get them on.
- **We don't sell your personal information.**
- We use service providers to run the game: sign-in, payments, hosting, analytics, and AI models that write, moderate, illustrate and narrate. **There are no ads.**
- You must be 13 or older to create an account.

## 1. What's public

As the [Terms of Use](./terms-of-use.md) explain, AI Wizard Duel is a public platform. The following is visible to anyone, including visitors who aren't signed in:

| Public | Who can see it |
| --- | --- |
| Username, display name, public profile | Anyone |
| Wizards: names, descriptions, portraits | Anyone |
| Duels and campaign battles: spells, narration, illustrations, results, replays | Anyone |
| Win/loss record, rankings, leaderboard positions | Anyone |
| Emoji reaction totals on spells | Signed-in users |
| Duel chat messages | Signed-in users watching the duel |
| How many people are watching a duel right now | Anyone (a count only, not who) |

Public content can be viewed, copied, shared, linked to and indexed by search engines, and it may stay in other people's copies even after it's removed from the Service.

## 2. What we collect

**Information you give us**

- **Account details:** your email address and name, through our sign-in provider (Clerk), or from Google or another account if you sign in with one. [Confirm which sign-in methods are enabled.]
- **Profile:** your username and display name.
- **Game content:** wizards, spells, chat messages and reactions.
- **Purchases:** which token bundles you buy and when. Stripe handles your card details; we never see or store your full card number.
- **Messages to us:** anything you send to support.

**Information created as you play**

- Duels, rounds, results, ratings, token balance and history, campaign progress, and invite/referral relationships.
- **Moderation records:** when content is rejected, we keep the rejected text, the reason and the category, so our team can review it and stop repeat abuse.
- **Notifications** we send you, and your notification settings.

**Information collected automatically**

- **Usage and device data:** pages viewed, features used, clicks, errors and crashes, browser and device type, approximate location from your IP address, and referring sites. We collect this with PostHog.
  - **If you're not signed in**, this is collected without cookies or browser storage, and without an identifier that follows you from visit to visit. We never record sessions for signed-out visitors.
  - **If you're signed in**, we link this data to your account, and we may record your session (session replay) so we can see how the game is used and find bugs. A replay captures what happens on the page, such as clicks, scrolling, and what's on screen. It masks everything you type, your account and email details, and your purchase history, and it never covers admin pages.
- **Watching data:** when you view a duel, we record that you viewed it and how often, to understand which duels are popular. For signed-out visitors, this uses a random ID stored in your browser. We also track who is in a duel "arena" right now, so we can show live watcher counts and tell players when their opponent is there.
- **Push subscriptions:** if you turn on notifications, your browser gives us an address for your device, which we use to send them.

**Cookies and similar technologies**

We use cookies and browser storage to keep you signed in (Clerk), to remember settings and your anonymous viewer ID, to remember whether analytics should run without cookies, and, for signed-in players, for analytics and session replay (PostHog). Signed-out visitors get no analytics cookies. We don't use advertising cookies. You can control cookies in your browser. ## 6. How long we keep it

- **Account information:** while your account is open. You can delete your account on your Edit Profile page. Your sign-in account is deleted right away, and the rest of your data within minutes. Copies in our backups are removed as those backups expire.
- **Duels with other players:** a duel belongs to everyone who played in it, so it stays after one player deletes their account, but it's no longer linked to them. Their username and profile are removed, their wizard is shown as a departed wizard, and their chat messages are deleted. Their spells, and the narration and illustrations made from them, stay so the other player's duel still makes sense. If any of it contains personal information, ask us and we'll remove it.
- **Campaign battles and unused wizards:** deleted along with the account.
- **Moderation records:** up to [12 months], or longer if needed for an ongoing safety issue. They're deleted along with the account.
- **Purchase records:** as long as tax and accounting law requires. If you delete your account, we keep the record of each purchase but remove its link to you.
- **Analytics:** according to our analytics settings, currently [X months].
- **Push subscriptions:** until you turn notifications off, or the device stops accepting them.
